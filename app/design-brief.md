# ACE Community Energy Website Design Brief

Design read: A trusted, optimistic civic-energy website for Ashford residents, community groups, schools, businesses, local organisations and site owners. It should feel local, practical and human rather than corporate or campaigning.

Concept spine: ACE Community Energy Loop. The website behaves like a living local energy loop in which people identify places, places become projects, projects create benefits, and those benefits increase participation.

Delivery tier: cinema. Scroll-led storytelling with a community-energy film, strong editorial typography, local imagery and restrained motion.

Theme paradigm: Pristine Light.
Background character: full-bleed cinematic imagery for the journey, cool civic paper and subtle material texture for content sections.
Typography character: clean expressive grotesk with rational mono details.
Hero architecture: cinematic image-first journey with restrained HTML copy.
Section system: asymmetric premium flow with editorial blocks and a project-status rail.
Signature components: layered image crop frames; vertical rhythm lines; off-grid editorial; horizontal project-status rail.
Narrative spine: living system / garden, expressed as the ACE Community Energy Loop.
Second-read moment: one oversized circular loop mark used as structure in the community-benefit section.

Locked palette:
- Civic paper #F4F7F3
- Deep ACE green #173F35
- Soft green surface #DDE9E0
- Muted ink #38544D
- Solar gold accent #D6A62A
Rationale: green carries community energy and landscape, while one warm solar accent gives the system energy without becoming a dark technical palette.

Locked type: Outfit for display and body, IBM Plex Mono for compact labels and project-status details.

Animation mode: animated-website
Journey shape: single-shot
Journey:
1. People. Local energy starts with people. Neighbours, organisations and local partners identify where cleaner energy can make a practical difference.
2. Place. Right here in Ashford. Rooftops, community buildings and local sites can become part of a more resilient energy system.
3. Project. Turn good sites into clean power. ACE develops renewable energy and efficiency projects around evidence, deliverability and community value.
4. Benefit. Keep the value local. Lower-carbon energy, resilience, local skills and future community benefit stay at the heart of each project.
5. Participation. Help shape what happens next. Suggest a site, partner with ACE, volunteer your skills or simply start a conversation.

World grammar: Photoreal South East England. Realistic community buildings and ordinary rooftops, mixed-age local participants, clean rooftop solar, soft natural daylight, restrained deep-green and solar-gold grade, stable exposure, no futuristic interfaces, no on-screen text.
Mobile framing: Keep people, building and solar focal points inside the centre 60 percent of the frame. Mobile gets a lighter encode and exact first-frame poster.
Delivery budget: desktop film <=32 MiB, mobile film <=16 MiB.

Homepage section plan:
1. Scroll journey: cinematic full viewport.
2. People to Place to Project to Benefit to Participation: off-grid editorial flow.
3. What ACE does: divided editorial rows.
4. Could your site work?: photographic site-qualification panel replacing decorative iconography.
5. Trust strip: evidence first, visible status, local benefit, no investment offer.
6. From idea to operation: Exploring, Assessing, Developing, Delivering, Operational. Horizontal status rail.
7. Community benefit: oversized loop structure with prose.
8. Get involved: stacked participation bands.
9. FAQ invitation: direct route into funding, site-hosting, community-benefit, participation and governance questions.
10. Contact invitation: full-width action field.
11. Footer: compact governance, policy and route links.

Persistent navigation: fixed at the top on every route, with a compact accessible mobile menu and a permanent Contact ACE action.

Site routes: /, /about, /what-we-do, /projects, /get-involved, /faqs, /governance, /privacy, /cookies, /accessibility, /website-terms, /contact.

Contact page:
- Welcomes residents, site owners, community organisations, schools, businesses and potential partners.
- Enquiry types: General enquiry, Suggest a site, Host a project, Partnership, Volunteer / skills, Privacy / data request, Complaint / feedback, Media / other.
- Fields: name, email, organisation optional, enquiry type, project or site postcode optional, message, privacy-notice acknowledgement.
- Labels above inputs, inline validation, clear success and failure states.
- No unconfirmed personal email address displayed. Submissions are stored securely in the site database until the final ACE mailbox is configured.
- A dedicated Suggest a Site panel asks for a postcode or location and enough context to follow up. It does not imply the full Ashford postcode solar-assessment engine is already live.

Asset plan: 16:9 storyboard, scroll film with desktop/mobile encodes and posters, community supporting imagery, custom energy icon sheet, civic texture, complete favicon/webmanifest/social head kit.

CTA inventory:
- Suggest a site: route-path underline interaction.
- Contact ACE: corner-bracket target interaction.
- See what we do: full-row shift interaction.
- Explore projects: mono status readout interaction.
- Get involved: circular loop interaction.
No shared global button class.

Content controls:
- Do not invent operational projects, investment offers, savings figures or community-return statistics.
- Keep statements grounded in ACE's community-benefit objects and project material.
- Future investment participation is described as an ambition, not an open offer.
- The complete Ashford postcode solar-assessment workflow remains a later phase until the authoritative active-postcode dataset is loaded.

Responsive behaviour: desktop nav one line under 80px; tablet preserves editorial hierarchy; mobile stacks content; reduced-motion visitors receive a complete static story with no video fetch.
